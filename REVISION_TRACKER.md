## Review procedure

1. Open only the problem statement.
2. Explain the input, output and constraints aloud.
3. Recall the approach and dry-run an example.
4. Implement from scratch in C++ or Python.
5. Test edge cases and submit.
6. Explain time and space complexity.
7. Record the outcome and next review date.

## Confidence

- Red: unable to recall the approach; retry tomorrow.
- Yellow: needed a hint or made a major mistake; retry within three days.
- Green: independently solved and explained; advance to the next interval.
