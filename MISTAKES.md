- [ ] Read input/output requirements and constraints.
- [ ] Dry-run a sample manually.
- [ ] Check minimum and maximum inputs.
- [ ] Check integer overflow and indexing.
- [ ] Check loop termination and off-by-one errors.
- [ ] Check time and space complexity.
- [ ] Test output formatting.
